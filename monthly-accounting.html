Monthly accounting / close packet

A close that ends in a management meeting, not a folder.

The monthly system is built around repeatable reconciliations, clean cut-off, a short management report, and a visible list of unresolved questions.

02.1

Close packet / sequence

working cadence
DAY 01–04

Collect + reconcile

Bank, card, loan, processor, payroll, and balance-sheet activity are matched and supported.

DAY 04–07

Cut off + close

Revenue, direct costs, accruals, deferred items, and open receivables are reviewed for the period.

DAY 07–09

Report + flag

Statements are assembled with variance notes and the short list of questions that need context.

DAY 09–10

Discuss + decide

Management reviews the drivers and assigns follow-up where cash, margin, tax, or process needs attention.

02.3

What arrives every month

core deliverables
  • P&L with current month, prior month, and operating notes
  • Balance sheet with supported key accounts
  • Cash position and near-term obligations
  • A/R and A/P review
  • Close question log and assigned follow-up
  • Management summary focused on material changes
The goal is not a thicker report. It is a dependable close, fewer unexplained balances, and a short list of numbers that can change a decision.
