Working principles / 01—04

A simple operating model.

01

Start with the business problem.

Before visual direction, get clear on what people need to understand, trust, compare, or do.

02

Give each company its own visual language.

The system should fit the business rather than forcing the business into a reusable agency template.

03

Design and front-end stay connected.

Responsive behavior, interaction, content, and implementation are treated as part of the design rather than a later translation.

04

Verify before calling it finished.

Real browser rendering, keyboard behavior, overflow, breakpoints, assets, and links all get checked before handoff.
