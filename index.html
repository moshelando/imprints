06 / Seasonal care

A finished garden is still moving.

Spring

Cutback, division, soil repair, drainage check.

Summer

Establishment watering, structural pruning, edits.

Autumn

Bulbs, transplanting, leaf management, stone reset.

Winter

Plan review, woody structure, next-season work.
