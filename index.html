What Lando Works builds

One practice.
Four disciplines.

01
Digital presence

Websites with a job to do.

Strategy, structure, visual design, content direction, and front-end development brought together in one coherent build.

  • Company and product sites
  • Campaigns and launches
  • Responsive front-end
  • Accessibility and performance
02
Bespoke systems

Software shaped around your company.

Purpose-built applications for workflows that off-the-shelf software cannot quite fit—designed around your people, processes, and existing systems.

  • Cross-platform applications
  • Internal tools and portals
  • Workflow automation
  • Data and system integrations
03
Publishing

Documents engineered for serious reading.

Professional and academic typesetting for material that must stay clear, consistent, and dependable from the first page to the last.

  • Papers, theses, and manuscripts
  • Reports, manuals, and course material
  • Complex equations, figures, and tables
  • Reusable LaTeX production systems
04
Digital environments

Screens and portals that connect the room.

Integrated digital touchpoints for physical spaces and day-to-day operations, from a single automated display to a connected information system.

  • Interactive and automated displays

  • Authenticated login portals
  • Dashboards and content-fed interfaces

How the work moves

From brief to finished.

  1. 01
    Understand

    Define the audience, the workflow, the constraints, and what a useful result must actually change.

  2. 02
    Architect

    Shape the content, system, information model, and integration plan before surface polish gets in the way.

  3. 03
    Build

    Design and develop the working experience, testing the critical paths as the project takes form.

  4. 04
    Integrate

    Connect the finished work with the tools, data, people, and environments already around it.

  5. 05
    Refine

    Finish the details, document the system, and leave room for the work to grow after launch.
