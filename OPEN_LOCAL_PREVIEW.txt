LANDO WORKS — LOCAL PREVIEW

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open a terminal in the extracted folder.
3. Run: `python3 -m http.server 4173`
4. Open: `http://localhost:4173/`

Opening `index.html` directly also works for the main page, but a local server
is the reliable way to review all links and project pages.

The four featured website demonstrations are included under projects/ and open from
the main Lando Works page.

Forms in the demonstrations are interface previews. They do not transmit or
store information.
