How we think about care

You do not arrive as a symptom. You arrive with a body, a schedule, a history, a threshold for change, and a life already in motion. We begin there. Each visit is shaped by what you tell us, what we notice together, and what happened after the last one.

The aim is not to build dependence on appointments. It is to help you feel more capable between them.

What a first visit feels like

Unhurried at the beginning. Clear by the end.

We start with conversation, not a checklist delivered at speed. You can tell us what hurts, what has been tried, what you are worried about, and what you hope will feel different.

Before you leave, you will know what we noticed, what we did, what to pay attention to afterward, and whether another visit makes sense.
