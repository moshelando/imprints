First Visit

There is time to arrive.

Your first appointment is 75 minutes. Nothing important needs to be squeezed into the first five.

00–15

We listen first.

Tell us what brings you in, what has changed, what has already been tried, and what would make the visit feel worthwhile. You do not need a polished history.

15–25

We make a small map.

Depending on why you are here, we may look at movement, tenderness, range, breathing, or other simple observations. We explain what we are checking as we go.

25–65

We use the visit.

Your appointment may include acupuncture, bodywork, movement, or a combination that fits your goals and your comfort. Consent stays active throughout the visit; you can change your mind at any point.

65–75

We close the loop.

Before you leave, we name what changed, what to notice afterward, and whether another visit makes sense. If there is something useful to do at home, we keep it small.

Before you come

Comfortable clothes are enough.

Eat and hydrate normally. Bring any information you think matters, but there is no paperwork scavenger hunt. If you have accessibility, sensory, or positioning needs, tell us when you book so we can prepare the room.

You are always welcome to ask what we are doing, decline part of a session, request more explanation, or stop.

The room

Quiet, private, and not precious.

We keep the space calm without making it ceremonial. You can talk, be quiet, ask questions, or take a minute before moving on.

Unsure whether Kindred is the right place?

The booking form includes space to tell us what is going on. If your needs are outside our scope, use the booking notes to record that context; this local site will not pretend to send it anywhere.
