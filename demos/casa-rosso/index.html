The house

Food first.
Fuss last.

The room has changed. The point of the room hasn’t.

Casa Rosso began as twelve tables, a chalkboard menu, and a kitchen too small for the number of people who came through the door. We kept the habit of cooking like a neighborhood place even after the room got bigger.
