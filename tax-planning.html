Tax planning / operating calendar

Tax planning should happen while there are still choices to make.

We keep tax decisions connected to owner compensation, distributions, cash reserves, entity activity, and the operating events already visible in the monthly books.

Planning is not return preparation.

Return preparation records the year after it has happened. Planning uses the books and forecast during the year so owners know what is changing and which decisions still have timing flexibility.

Tax planning starts with books that can be trusted.

If the monthly close is still unstable, we begin there and layer tax planning onto the same operating calendar.
