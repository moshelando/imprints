Transaction advisory

Independent support from process design through negotiation.

We advise on acquisitions, dispositions, partnerships and structured transactions involving real assets. The objective is not simply to run a process; it is to shape one that reflects the client's strategic position, information advantage and negotiating leverage.

Process and transaction strategy
Counterparty identification and outreach
Valuation and commercial framing

REIT & platform advisory

REIT strategy and portfolio narrative

Capital allocation frameworks

Environmental & energy market advisory

Commercial decisions shaped by policy, infrastructure and physical constraints.

Market assessment and opportunity mapping
