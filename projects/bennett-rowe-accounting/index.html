04 / FIT

Built for the point between bookkeeping and a finance department.

qualification

Usually a good fit

  • Owner-led business with real operating complexity
  • Multiple revenue streams, accounts, processors, or projects
  • Monthly decisions depend on cash, margin, or receivables
  • Internal staff needs accounting structure or controller judgment

Usually not the right fit

  • Only an annual tax return is needed
  • No monthly books are maintained
  • Every accounting process must remain owner-dependent
  • A full-time CFO or transaction team is the immediate need
