Security

Your financial data is not the product.

Pennywise is designed around a simple standard: collect less, protect what is needed, and explain every permission clearly.

✓
Read-only account connectionsNo money movementNo sale of financial data
Principles

Trust should be understandable.

Minimize

Use only the information required to create the financial picture.

Protect

Design connections and stored information around strong, layered safeguards.

Explain

Make permissions, controls, and data use clear enough to make an informed choice.

Product controls

Protection where it changes the risk.

01

Read-only by design

Connections would read balances and activity; they would not authorize Pennywise to move money.

02

Clear sessions

Devices and active sessions would be reviewable, with reauthentication for sensitive changes.

03

Smallest useful data set

Features are designed around the least information needed, not collection for its own sake.

04

Export and disconnect

The product model keeps control visible: export the plan or remove a connection without hunting.
