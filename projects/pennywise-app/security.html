Security

Your financial data is not the product.

Security is easier to trust when the model is easy to see: limited access, encrypted handling, and clear user control.

01 · minimize

Use only what the feature needs.

Pennywise is designed to organize financial information without requiring transaction authority.

02 · protect

Encrypt data in transit and at rest.

Connections and stored product data are designed around encrypted handling and access controls.

03 · explain

Make permissions understandable.

Data use and browser privacy choices are written in ordinary language, with settings you can revisit.

Control

You should be able to leave with your data.

Pennywise’s product model includes export and account-disconnection controls rather than treating connection as permanent.
