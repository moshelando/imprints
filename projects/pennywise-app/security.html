Security

Your money should help you — not become someone else's product.

Pennywise is built to help you understand your financial position while keeping control of your information with you.

What Pennywise uses.

Pennywise uses the financial information needed to create your picture: balances, activity, commitments, and goals.

What Pennywise does not do.

Connections are read-only. Pennywise does not move your money and does not sell financial information.

Designed around your control.

Privacy is part of the product design, not an afterthought. You should understand what is used and why.
