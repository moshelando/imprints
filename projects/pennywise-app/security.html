Security

Your financial data is not the product.

Pennywise exists to help you understand your money, not to use your information for someone else's benefit.

What Pennywise accesses.

Linked accounts provide the information needed to understand balances, transactions, and commitments.

What Pennywise does not do.

Connections are read-only. Pennywise cannot move your money and does not sell financial information.

Privacy by design.

The service is built around collecting only what is needed, explaining how information is used, and keeping control with you.
