Privacy

Your money data stays yours.

Pennywise is built around data minimization and user control. This static site uses essential browser storage for privacy choices and, if you complete the local onboarding, the setup choices you ask it to remember.

Essential browser storage

Essential storage supports privacy settings and the optional local onboarding state. It does not send that information to a server.

Optional analytics

The cookie control lets you record an analytics preference. No third-party analytics service is configured on this website.

Financial connections

This website does not connect to financial institutions. Product copy describes the intended Pennywise product model, including read-only financial connections and user-controlled disconnection.

Your control

You can revisit cookie settings from the footer. Browser storage can also be cleared using your browser’s site-data controls.
