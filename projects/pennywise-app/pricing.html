Can I switch plans?

Yes. Move between Free and Plus without rebuilding your spending plan.

Is there a contract?

No annual commitment is required. Plus is billed monthly and can be canceled before the next renewal.

What happens to my data?

You can export your plan data and disconnect linked accounts at any time.

No. Pennywise does not make money by selling loans, cards, or investment products inside the app.
