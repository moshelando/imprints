Atelier

Construction is the decoration.

Patterns are developed in the Paris studio and adjusted directly on the body. Jackets are half-canvassed; hems and critical seams are finished by hand; fabric is sourced from mills in France, Italy, and Japan.

We release fewer garments so the cut can do more work.

Paris Small runs Natural fibers Repair service

The aim is not minimalism. It is concentration.

The work

Cut. Fit. Finish.

01Cut

Patterns are established on the body first, then resolved for repeatable production in small runs.

02Fit

Balance, line, and movement are adjusted in fittings rather than corrected with surface detail.

03Finish

Hand work is concentrated where it changes drape, durability, or the way a garment feels in use.

Aftercare

Keep the piece in service.

Maison Noire offers alterations and repair for house garments. Client services can advise on pressing, storage, and restoration before work is scheduled.
