Our Story

A room that grew without becoming a theme.

Casa Rosso opened in 1988 with twelve tables, a chalkboard, and a kitchen that was already too small for the number of people who came through the door.

“The first rule was that it had to feel like somewhere we would actually eat every week.”

The cooking started with Rome: fried artichokes, amatriciana, cacio e pepe, roast meats, bitter greens. Emilia-Romagna arrived through fresh pasta. The rest came from the neighborhood and the market.

That remains the shape of the kitchen. Buy well. Make the pasta here. Let ingredients stay recognizable. Leave enough room in the evening for conversation.

A few dates
1988

Twelve tables, one room.

Casa Rosso opens on Via del Porto. The menu is written on a chalkboard and changes with the morning market.

2004

The wall comes down.

The neighboring storefront becomes part of the dining room. More tables arrive; the menu stays short.

Today

The afternoon still belongs to pasta.

Every service begins the same way: dough on the bench, sauce on the stove, wine opened for the staff to taste before the doors do.
