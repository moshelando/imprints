About Papertrail

A magazine about things that remain interesting after the scroll ends.

We report on the designed and inhabited world at human scale.

Papertrail is an independent editorial project covering cities, objects, food, work, and useful obsessions. We like reporting that starts with a place, a person, or a thing you can point to.

No daily content quota. No trend desk. Fewer stories, worked harder.

Editorial principles

Go there. Talk to the person who does the work. Photograph the real thing. Explain the system without draining the life out of it.
