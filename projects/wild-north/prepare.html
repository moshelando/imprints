CHAPTER 01

Weather system

Layer for work and for stopping. A jacket that feels warm while climbing may be useless at camp; a warm camp layer buried beneath everything is nearly as bad.

KEEP REACHABLE
Shell, hat, gloves
KEEP DRY
Sleep clothes, insulation
CHAPTER 02

Wet system

Assume rain can outlast motivation. Use nested waterproofing for sleep insulation and electronics; accept that tents, boots, and rainwear will become field-wet.

RIVER ROUTES
Dry bags inside travel bags
FOOT ROUTES
Pack liner + internal dry bags
CHAPTER 03

Load system

The number on a scale matters less than whether you can carry it steadily over irregular ground for a full day. Train with the shoes and pack you will actually use.

BROOKS
12–16 kg starting range
BASECAMP
Day load after arrival
