Sector context

Four areas of focus.

The same advisory disciplines take different forms across markets. Northline concentrates on sectors where physical assets, regulation, operating realities and capital markets frequently meet.

Real Estate

Operating assets, portfolios, development, adaptive reuse, specialty real estate and asset-level strategic alternatives.

Environmental Markets

Environmental credits, compliance markets, mitigation, land-use value, regulatory economics and emerging monetization models.

Energy Infrastructure

Power, generation, storage, transmission-adjacent assets, industrial infrastructure and energy-linked real estate.

REITs & Real Asset Platforms

Public and private real-asset companies navigating capital allocation, portfolio composition, investor expectations and strategic change.
