Market note / August 2026

Real assets are being repriced by more than the cost of capital.

A short Northline observation on operating constraints, infrastructure demand and portfolio strategy.
Read briefing note

Higher financing costs remain important, but they are only one layer of the current reset. Power availability, permitting timelines, insurance, land-use constraints and operating intensity increasingly determine which assets can support a durable investment thesis. For owners, that makes portfolio strategy more specific: the question is not simply what an asset is worth today, but which constraints can be changed, which cannot, and how the answer should affect capital allocation.
