Services

One landscape, fewer handoffs.

Design, construction, planting, and the details that connect them stay coordinated as one scope.

01

Landscape Design

Planning for new landscapes, major renovations, and spaces that need a clear direction before construction begins.

Scope may include
  • Site assessment and base planning
  • Concept layouts and spatial planning
  • Planting and material palettes
  • Lighting and irrigation direction
  • Phasing and construction scope
02

Construction

Site work and installation coordinated from earthwork through the finished landscape.

Scope may include
  • Grading and drainage
  • Patios, walks, and garden walls
  • Steps, edging, and gravel work
  • Planting and soil preparation
  • Low-voltage lighting
03

Planting & Garden Care

Planting installation and selective ongoing care for landscapes that benefit from a consistent hand.

Scope may include
  • Planting installation and establishment
  • Spring and fall garden work
  • Structural pruning
  • Perennial division and editing
  • Seasonal planting and garden review
