Services

One landscape, fewer handoffs.

Design, site work, planting and finish details stay connected from the first walk-through to the final punch list.

01

Design

For new landscapes, major renovations and spaces that need a clear plan before construction begins.

  • Site assessment + measured base plan
  • Concept plans and layout options
  • Planting and material palettes
  • Lighting and irrigation direction
  • Phasing and construction budget
02

Build

Construction and installation with one team coordinating the sequence from earthwork through planting.

  • Grading and drainage
  • Patios, walks and garden walls
  • Steps, edging and gravel work
  • Planting and soil preparation
  • Low-voltage lighting
03

Maintain

Selective ongoing care for landscapes we build and gardens that need a more thoughtful seasonal rhythm.

  • Spring and fall cleanup
  • Structural pruning
  • Perennial division and editing
  • Seasonal planting
  • Garden health review
Material approach

Use fewer materials. Use them well.

We favor stone, gravel, timber, steel and planting that weather honestly and can be repaired without rebuilding the whole space.
