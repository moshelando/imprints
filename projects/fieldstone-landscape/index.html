The process

Clear enough to plan around.

Every project moves through the same four checkpoints, with decisions and costs made visible before the work moves ahead.

01

Walk the site

Light, slope, drainage, access, existing plants and how you use the space.

02

Draw the plan

Layout, materials, planting and the scope that best fits the budget.

03

Build in sequence

Site work first, hardscape next, planting and finish work last.

04

Grow it in

Care notes, seasonal follow-up and adjustments as the landscape establishes.

Built around the site

The practical things shape the good parts.

Drainage, sun, access, soil, privacy, maintenance, and budget are part of the design from the first sketch—not problems left for the crew to solve later.

Water

Grade and drainage are resolved before new surfaces and planting go in.

Light

Planting plans follow the actual sun pattern rather than a generic palette.

Access

Construction sequence is planned around gates, neighbors, stairs, and occupied homes.

Care

Plant choices and details reflect how much seasonal work the landscape can realistically support.
