Case 01

Birch Court

A quiet stone room set into a narrow rear garden.

Site
Narrow residential rear yard
Ground
Clay over compacted fill
Scope
Drainage, stone, planting, lighting
Season
Built spring · planted autumn

Existing condition

The yard moved water toward the house and had no useful level place to sit.

The project began with drainage and grade. A narrow permeable walk moved circulation to the side, leaving enough width for one simple court. Stone walls hold the new elevations while the planting softens the geometry over time.

Plan + materials

The structure stays quiet so the planting can change.

  • Split fieldstone retaining edges
  • Permeable gravel sub-base
  • Large-format thermal bluestone
  • Birch, sedge, fern and hydrangea

Transformation

Water first.
Finish second.

The visible work follows the invisible work below grade.
