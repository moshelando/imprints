Care

Start with what is asking for attention.

Our services are different tools inside one approach: listen closely, use only what is useful, and keep the plan small enough to live with.

01

Therapeutic bodywork

Hands-on work for pain, tension, training load, recovery, and bodies that have been bracing for too long.

A session can be focused and specific or slower and broader. We use pressure, movement, positioning, and time according to what feels useful—not according to a fixed routine.

60 minutes · $145
90 minutes · $195

02

Acupuncture

A quiet private-room visit that begins with context and ends with time to settle.

We use acupuncture as part of an ongoing conversation about symptoms, sleep, stress, energy, digestion, training, and recovery. The treatment plan can change as your response changes.

First visit, 75 minutes · $165
Follow-up, 60 minutes · $145

There is no prize for doing the most. We would rather give you one useful thing to notice than six new tasks to manage.

03

Movement

A practical session for mobility, strength, breath, and confidence with movement.

We work with the movements that matter in your actual life. Sessions may include simple strength work, graded exposure, mobility, or breath—but the emphasis is always on making the next step clear enough to repeat.

50 minutes · $125

04

Integrated visits

Sometimes the most useful visit does not fit neatly inside one modality.

When clinically appropriate, a longer appointment can combine hands-on work, acupuncture, or movement. We will explain what we are suggesting and why before we begin.

Availability varies by practitioner.

You do not need to diagnose your own starting point.
